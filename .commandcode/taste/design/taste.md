# Design / Aesthetic Preferences

- Holds a very high aesthetic bar: wants "world-class" designs that are vibrant/energetic yet elegant, with careful decorative detail and polish. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers GIF/animated-image backgrounds over heavy CSS-animated effects when the latter degrade JS responsiveness/performance. Confidence: 0.8
- Cares about typography on canvas: never break words or information awkwardly across pages (respect hyphen/full-stop boundaries), apply emphasis to whole words rather than sub-word fragments, and re-measure layout to prevent text overflow. Confidence: 0.85
